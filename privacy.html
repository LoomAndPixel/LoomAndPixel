Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Loom & Pixel is a small creative studio. There are no accounts on this site, and we only collect your name and email if you choose to contact us.

Visits

We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies, to see how many people visit and which pages they view. We only see totals, not who you are. Google handles this data under its privacy policy.

Project videos and images

Project pages show videos from YouTube, Vimeo and ArtStation, and some images are loaded from ArtStation. When a page with one of these loads, that service sees your IP address and may set its own cookies; YouTube videos use its privacy-enhanced mode. Each service follows its own privacy policy.

Contact form and email

When you send the contact form, your name, email, and message are delivered to us through Web3Forms. We use what you send, and anything you email to us, only to reply and discuss your project. We don't sell it, share it, or add you to a mailing list.

Client reviews

Work in progress is shared with clients through a private Google Site and Google Drive, open only to the people we invite. Google checks that you're signed in and handles that data under its privacy policy.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's add-on. To see or delete anything you've sent us, just ask. Links to LinkedIn and other sites follow those sites' own policies.

Questions

Email us at Hello@LoomAndPixel.com. If anything here changes, we'll update this page.
