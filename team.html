Kurt Bernardin

Digital Creator & Founder

I’m a Los Angeles-based digital creator with over a decade of experience immersed in the world of digital art. Combining creative vision with technical expertise, my work spans 3D compositing, lighting, look development, and advanced animation systems.

Over the last ten years, I have produced high-quality content across commercials, marketing campaigns, television shows, digital collectibles, and immersive VR spaces. Alongside my studio execution at Loom & Pixel, leading a small, agile team of dedicated artists to deliver high-end visual pipelines, I serve as a community college professor, mentoring the next generation of aspiring artists and pushing the boundaries of what is visually possible.
